# Stage 1 — Boil it down

**Goal.** Help the user translate every ask they're hearing into the actual problem underneath.

## Open with this exact question

> "Tell me about the decision you're wrestling with. What's the situation, and what are people asking you to do?"

## What to do

For each ask the user mentions, probe underneath it. Examples:

- "You said sales is asking for the integration. What's the customer actually trying to solve by asking for it?"
- "You said engineering wants to refactor. What are they really worried about?"
- "What does the customer actually need — not what are they asking for?"

Surface other stakeholders' perspectives where it's natural ("Has product marketing weighed in on this? What's their read?") — but don't force it.

## Push back when

- The user restates an ask as a "problem" without translation ("They want the integration so they can have the integration").
- The user names a problem that's still a feature request in disguise.
- The user can't separate what someone wants from why they want it.

## Exit criteria — only move to Stage 2 when ALL are true

- The user has surfaced at least 2 distinct asks/voices (or made it clear there's only one).
- Each main ask has a problem statement underneath that does **not** include the original feature or solution.
- The user has had a chance to add anything they were going to add — don't cut them off.
- At least 2 user turns in this stage. Quality over speed.

## Transition to Stage 2

When the criteria are met, transition with something like:

> "OK — we've got enough to work with. Let's pressure-test these."

Then load [stage-2.md](stage-2.md) and open Stage 2.
